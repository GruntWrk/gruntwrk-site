# GruntWrk - Complete Product Overview

## What is GruntWrk?

GruntWrk is a local services marketplace that connects customers who need work done with service providers who do the work. It covers home and commercial services like cleaning, repairs, plumbing, electrical, painting, moving, furniture assembly, outdoor maintenance, mounting/installation, and junk removal/disposal.

The platform is live at https://www.gruntwrk.com and currently in Early Access, with iOS and Android apps coming soon.

## The Core Problem GruntWrk Solves

Most local service marketplaces charge providers heavily just to find work. Providers pay lead fees, buy credit packs, pay subscriptions, and purchase visibility boosts. These costs get passed directly to customers through inflated quotes. Customers end up paying more, and providers keep less of what they earn.

GruntWrk eliminates all of those middleman fees. The only fee is a 15% platform payment fee when a job is completed and paid through the platform.

## How GruntWrk Works

### For Customers:
1. Describe what you need - Post your request, location, and budget
2. Choose who to hire - Receive quotes from matched providers or book directly with a provider of your choice
3. Manage the job in one place - Use your workbench to message, confirm the provider, follow payment, and rebook trusted providers

### For Providers:
1. Build a public profile with your skills, availability, and service areas
2. Receive direct requests from customers - no paying to unlock leads
3. Quote on jobs, message customers, take payment, and manage rebookings from one workbench

## Key Value Propositions

### For Customers:
- **Affordable prices**: Providers don't pay lead fees, credit packs, or subscriptions. That means more affordable quotes for customers.
- **Hire with confidence**: See every job a provider has completed, read what other customers said, and check their ratings before hiring.
- **Simple, secure payments**: Pay half upfront, half when the job is done. The platform handles everything. No cash, no transfers, no hassle.

### For Providers:
- No pay-per-lead or contact unlock fees
- No credit packs, subscriptions, or boost charges
- Public profile, availability, and direct requests
- Quotes, messaging, payment steps, and repeat bookings in one workbench
- Your customers pay less because you don't pay middleman fees

## The GruntWrk Fee Model

GruntWrk charges only ONE fee: a 15% platform payment fee when a job is completed.

Compare this to typical marketplaces that charge:
- Registration fees (e.g., Taskrabbit)
- Lead or contact fees (e.g., Fixando, Zaask, Bark)
- Credit packs (e.g., Fixando, Zaask, Bark)
- Visibility boosts (e.g., Fixando, Checkatrade)
- Subscriptions or memberships (e.g., Bark, Checkatrade)
- Shortlist or acceptance fees (e.g., MyBuilder)
- Payment-processing add-on fees (e.g., Fixando, Taskrabbit, Checkatrade)
- Payout reserves or holdbacks (e.g., Fixando)

All of those are FREE on GruntWrk. The only charge is the 15% platform payment fee.

## The Workbench

The GruntWrk workbench is the central hub where both customers and providers manage everything:
- **Compare quotes clearly**: Providers compete on quality and price, not on who paid more for visibility. See options side by side.
- **Book directly when ready**: Already know who you want? Send a request straight to that provider. Check their ratings and previous jobs first.
- **Keep everything together**: Messages, progress, payment, and reviews live inside the job workbench. One place for the full history.
- **Pay and rebook with confidence**: Pay half upfront, half when the job is done. No cash, no transfers. The platform handles it all.
