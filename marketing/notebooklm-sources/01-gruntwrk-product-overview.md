# GruntWrk - Complete Product Overview

## What is GruntWrk?

GruntWrk is a request-first quote coordination platform for local service providers. Customers describe the job once, review providers GruntWrk can contact for them, compare quotes in one place, and manage the work through a shared workbench. It covers home and commercial services like cleaning, repairs, plumbing, electrical, painting, moving, furniture assembly, outdoor maintenance, mounting/installation, and junk removal/disposal.

The platform is live at https://www.gruntwrk.com and currently in Early Access, with iOS and Android apps coming soon.

## The Core Problem GruntWrk Solves

Most local service platforms charge providers heavily just to find work. Providers pay lead fees, buy credit packs, pay subscriptions, and purchase visibility boosts. These costs get passed directly to customers through inflated quotes. Customers end up paying more, and providers keep less of what they earn.

GruntWrk eliminates all of those middleman fees. Customers pay providers directly — cash, MB Way, bank transfer, whatever works. The provider then pays GruntWrk a small 10% job fee separately. No money flows through the platform. No payout delays.

## How GruntWrk Works

### For Customers:
1. Describe what you need - Post your request, location, and budget
2. Choose who to hire - Receive quotes from matched providers or book directly with a provider of your choice
3. Manage the job in one place - Use your workbench to message, confirm the provider, track progress, and rebook trusted providers

### For Providers:
1. Build a public profile with your skills, availability, and service areas
2. Receive direct requests from customers - no paying to unlock leads
3. Quote on jobs, message customers, get paid directly, and manage rebookings from one workbench

## Key Value Propositions

### For Customers:
- **Affordable prices**: Providers don't pay lead fees, credit packs, or subscriptions. That means more affordable quotes for customers.
- **Hire with confidence**: See every job a provider has completed, read what other customers said, and check their ratings before hiring.
- **Pay providers directly**: Pay your provider however you prefer — cash, MB Way, or bank transfer. No platform middleman, no payout delays, no payment holds.

### For Providers:
- No pay-per-lead or contact unlock fees
- No credit packs, subscriptions, or boost charges
- Public profile, availability, and direct requests
- Customers pay you directly — cash, MB Way, or bank transfer. No payout delays.
- Quotes, messaging, and repeat bookings in one workbench
- Only a 10% job fee paid separately — your customers pay less because you don't pay middleman fees

## The GruntWrk Fee Model

GruntWrk charges only ONE fee: a 10% provider job fee when a job is completed. Customers pay providers directly — cash, MB Way, bank transfer — and providers pay GruntWrk the 10% job fee separately. No money flows through the platform. No payout delays.

Compare this to typical platforms that charge:
- Registration fees (e.g., Taskrabbit)
- Lead or contact fees (e.g., Fixando, Zaask, Bark)
- Credit packs (e.g., Fixando, Zaask, Bark)
- Visibility boosts (e.g., Fixando, Checkatrade)
- Subscriptions or memberships (e.g., Bark, Checkatrade)
- Shortlist or acceptance fees (e.g., MyBuilder)
- Payment-processing add-on fees (e.g., Fixando, Taskrabbit, Checkatrade)
- Payout reserves or holdbacks (e.g., Fixando) — on GruntWrk, customers pay you directly so there are no payout delays

All of those are FREE on GruntWrk. The only charge is the 10% provider job fee.

## The Workbench

The GruntWrk workbench is the central hub where both customers and providers manage everything:
- **Compare quotes clearly**: Providers compete on quality and price, not on who paid more for visibility. See options side by side.
- **Book directly when ready**: Already know who you want? Send a request straight to that provider. Check their ratings and previous jobs first.
- **Keep everything together**: Messages, progress, and reviews live inside the job workbench. One place for the full history.
- **Pay and rebook with confidence**: Pay your provider directly — cash, MB Way, or bank transfer. No platform middleman holding your money.
