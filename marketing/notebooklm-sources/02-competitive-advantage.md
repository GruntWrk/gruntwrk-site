# GruntWrk Competitive Advantage and Market Positioning

## The Industry Problem

The local services industry has a broken fee model. Here's what typically happens on other platforms:

1. A homeowner needs a plumber
2. They post a request on a platform like Bark, Fixando, or Zaask
3. Plumbers on these platforms have already paid registration fees, monthly subscriptions, and credit packs just to be listed
4. When the job request comes in, plumbers pay AGAIN to unlock the customer's contact details or to submit a quote
5. To recoup all these costs, plumbers inflate their prices
6. The homeowner ends up paying more than they should
7. The plumber keeps less than they should
8. The platform profits from both sides

This creates a lose-lose situation for the two people who matter most: the customer and the service provider. The only winner is the platform.

## How GruntWrk Fixes This

GruntWrk flips the model. Instead of charging providers at every step of the journey, GruntWrk only charges once: a 10% provider job fee when a job is completed. Customers pay providers directly — cash, MB Way, bank transfer — and providers pay GruntWrk the 10% job fee separately. No money flows through the platform. No payout delays.

This means:
- Providers don't inflate their prices to cover platform costs
- Customers get genuinely cheaper quotes
- Providers keep more of what they earn — and get paid instantly by the customer
- No payout holdbacks or waiting for platform releases
- The platform only makes money when real work gets done

## Detailed Fee Comparison

### Registration / Onboarding
- **Competitors**: Some charge a one-time registration fee before providers can start working (e.g., Taskrabbit)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Lead or Contact Fees
- **Competitors**: Providers pay to unlock customer details or spend money each time they pursue a job (e.g., Fixando, Zaask, Bark)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Credit Packs
- **Competitors**: Providers must prepay for credit packs before they can quote on requests (e.g., Fixando, Zaask, Bark)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Visibility Boosts
- **Competitors**: Pay extra to appear higher in search or get more profile exposure (e.g., Fixando, Checkatrade)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Subscriptions or Memberships
- **Competitors**: Monthly platform access fees just to stay listed (e.g., Bark, Checkatrade)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Shortlist or Acceptance Fees
- **Competitors**: Charged when a customer shows interest (e.g., MyBuilder)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Payment Processing Add-ons
- **Competitors**: Extra payment fees on top of platform fees (e.g., Fixando, Taskrabbit, Checkatrade)
- **GruntWrk**: Free

### Payout Reserves
- **Competitors**: A portion of provider funds held before payout (e.g., Fixando)
- **GruntWrk**: Not applicable — customers pay providers directly, so there are no payout delays or holdbacks

### Job Fee
- **Competitors**: Transaction fee on completed bookings (e.g., Fixando, Taskrabbit, Checkatrade)
- **GruntWrk**: 10% provider job fee (the ONLY fee) — paid separately by the provider, not deducted from customer payment
