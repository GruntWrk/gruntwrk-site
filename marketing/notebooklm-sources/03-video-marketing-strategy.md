# GruntWrk Video Marketing Strategy and Content Guide

## Video Content Opportunities

GruntWrk's messaging naturally lends itself to compelling video content. The platform has two distinct audiences (customers and service providers) and a clear competitive differentiator (no middleman fees, direct payment, no payout delays), making it ideal for targeted video marketing.

## Audience Segments

### Audience 1: Customers (Service Requesters)
- Homeowners needing repairs, cleaning, plumbing, electrical work, etc.
- People moving house who need help with packing, lifting, assembly
- Anyone who has been frustrated by overpriced quotes from other platforms
- People who want transparency in pricing and provider quality

### Audience 2: Service Providers (Tradespeople)
- Independent plumbers, electricians, cleaners, painters, handymen
- Small service businesses looking for new customers
- Providers frustrated with paying lead fees on other platforms
- Tradespeople who want to build their own client base without middleman costs

## Video Topic Ideas

### Explainer Videos (Both Audiences)
1. **"How GruntWrk Works"** - The 3-step process: describe what you need, choose who to hire, manage the job
2. **"Why GruntWrk is Different"** - The fee comparison story, showing how other platforms charge at every step
3. **"The GruntWrk Workbench"** - Demo of the central hub for quotes, messages, payments, and rebooking

### Customer-Focused Videos
4. **"Why Your Quotes Are So Expensive"** - Exposing how lead fees inflate prices on other platforms
5. **"Hire With Confidence"** - How ratings, reviews, and job history help customers make informed decisions
6. **"Pay Your Provider Directly"** - No platform middleman: pay cash, MB Way, or bank transfer. No payout delays.
7. **"Find Help for Any Job"** - Showcasing the 10 service categories

### Provider-Focused Videos
8. **"Stop Paying to Chase Leads"** - The provider value proposition: no fees to find and win work
9. **"Build Your Service Business on GruntWrk"** - Profile, availability, direct requests, and rebooking
10. **"Keep More of What You Earn"** - Only a 10% job fee vs. the stack of charges on other platforms. Get paid directly by customers — no payout delays.
11. **"Your Workbench, Your Business"** - Managing quotes, messages, payments, and repeat customers

### Social Media Short-Form Content
12. **"Did you know?"** series - Quick facts about hidden fees in the industry
13. **Customer testimonial clips** - Early access reviews animated or narrated
14. **Before/After fee comparison** - Visual breakdown of what a provider pays elsewhere vs. GruntWrk
15. **"Public local jobs and providers. Browse first."** - Brand message clips

## Key Messages for Video Scripts

### The Money Message (Customers)
"When providers aren't charged to find work, they don't need to inflate their prices. That saving goes straight to you."

### The Freedom Message (Providers)
"Run your service business without paying to chase leads. Build a public profile, share your availability, receive direct requests, compare quotes, message customers, get paid directly, and rebook repeat work from one workbench."

### The Trust Message (Both)
"See every job a provider has completed, read what other customers said, and check their ratings before you hire."

### The Direct Payment Message (Both)
"Pay your provider directly — cash, MB Way, or bank transfer. No platform middleman. No payout delays. Your money goes straight to the person doing the work."

### The Fairness Message (Brand)
"No pay-per-lead. No credit packs. No subscriptions. No visibility boosts. Only a 10% provider job fee."

## NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompts

When generating Audio Overviews in NotebookLM, consider these angles:

1. **"Discuss how GruntWrk disrupts local services by eliminating provider fees and letting customers pay providers directly"** - This will generate a compelling podcast-style discussion about the business model.

2. **"Explain the customer journey on GruntWrk from posting a request to completing a job"** - Perfect for an explainer video voiceover.

3. **"Compare the fee structures of traditional local service platforms with GruntWrk's direct-payment approach"** - Great for a competitive comparison video.

4. **"Discuss why service providers should switch to GruntWrk from platforms like Bark, Fixando, or Taskrabbit"** - Targeted at the provider audience.

5. **"What makes GruntWrk different for homeowners looking for affordable local services?"** - Targeted at the customer audience.

## Video Production Notes

- GruntWrk brand color: green (#22c55e) on cream/warm white background
- Clean, modern aesthetic, no heavy effects
- Tone: Direct, honest, practical, no marketing fluff
- The platform is in Early Access, so emphasize "get in early" and "growing community"
- Apps are coming to iOS and Android soon, which adds urgency
- Currently supports English and Portuguese markets
